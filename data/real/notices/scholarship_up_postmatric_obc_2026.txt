# Uttar Pradesh Post-Matric Scholarship & Fee Reimbursement Scheme for OBC Category

GOVERNMENT OF UTTAR PRADESH
Department of Backward Classes Welfare
Notification No: BCW/2026/PM-OBC/982

SUBJECT: Post-Matric Scholarship and Fee Reimbursement Online Application for OBC Category Students for Academic Session 2026-2027.

1. SCHEME OVERVIEW:
Applications are invited from eligible Other Backward Class (OBC) students studying in recognized schools, colleges, and technical institutes in Uttar Pradesh in Class 11, Class 12, ITI, Polytechnic Diploma, Undergraduate, and Postgraduate programs.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Category: Must belong to recognized Other Backward Classes (OBC) of Uttar Pradesh.
- Education: Must have passed Class 10 (Secondary School) or equivalent from a recognized Board.
- Age: Minimum age 15 years, Maximum age 35 years as on 1st July 2026.
- Annual Family Income: Total family income from all sources must not exceed Rs. 2,00,000 (2.0 Lakhs per annum).
- State/Domicile: Resident of Uttar Pradesh.

3. IMPORTANT DATES:
- Online Registration Opens: 15 September 2026
- Last Date for Online Application: 31 December 2026
- Last Date to Submit Hard Copy to Institute: 10 January 2027
- Scholarship & Fee Reimbursement Disbursal: February 2027

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Domicile Certificate of Uttar Pradesh (UP Niwas Praman Patra) [Typical issue time: 20 days]
- Caste Certificate (OBC Jati Praman Patra issued by Tehsildar) [Typical issue time: 30 days]
- Income Certificate (Aay Praman Patra issued by competent authority) [Typical issue time: 15 days]
- Class 10 Marksheet / Passing Certificate
- Aadhaar Card linked with Bank Account (NPCI Direct Benefit Transfer seeded)
- Current Year College/School Fee Receipt and Bonafide Certificate

5. APPLICATION MODE & FEES:
- Application Mode: Online portal only at https://scholarship.up.gov.in
- Application Fee: Nil (Rs. 0 / Free for all candidates)

6. STEP-BY-STEP APPLICATION PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Student Registration on UP Scholarship Portal (scholarship.up.gov.in) using Aadhaar and High School roll number.
Step 2: Complete student login and fill personal, academic, and bank account details.
Step 3: Upload scanned copies of income, caste, domicile certificates, and fee receipt.
Step 4: Verify biometric e-KYC using Aadhaar OTP.
Step 5: Lock final application and take a printout of the computer-generated acknowledgement.
Step 6: Submit the signed printout along with self-attested document copies to your school/college office before the deadline.
