# Staff Selection Commission (SSC) - Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination 2026

STAFF SELECTION COMMISSION
Government of India, Department of Personnel and Training
Notice No. 3/1/2026-P&P-I

RECRUITMENT NOTICE: CONSTABLE (GD) EXAMINATION 2026

1. VACANCY DETAILS & ORGANIZATIONS:
Recruitment of Constable (General Duty) in Border Security Force (BSF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), Assam Rifles (AR), and Secretariat Security Force (SSF).

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Nationality: Citizen of India.
- Educational Qualification: The candidate must have passed Matriculation or 10th Class Examination from a recognized Board/University as on the closing date.
- Age Limit: 18 to 23 years as on 01-08-2026. Permissible upper age relaxations: OBC (3 years up to 26 years), SC/ST (5 years up to 28 years).
- Gender: Open to both Male and Female candidates.
- Physical Standards: Height Male 170 cms (162.5 cms for ST), Height Female 157 cms (150 cms for ST).

3. IMPORTANT DATES:
- Dates for submission of online applications: 01-10-2026 to 15-11-2026
- Last date and time for receipt of online applications: 15-11-2026 (23:00)
- Last date and time for making online fee payment: 16-11-2026 (23:00)
- Dates of 'Window for Application Form Correction': 20-11-2026 to 22-11-2026
- Schedule of Computer Based Examination (CBE): January - February 2027

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- 10th Class Passing Certificate and Marksheet (Matriculation proof of Date of Birth)
- Valid Photo Identity Card (Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Driving License)
- Caste / Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) if claiming reservation [Lead time: 30 days]
- Domicile Certificate / Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) of concerned State [Lead time: 20 days]
- Recent passport size photograph and scanned signature

5. APPLICATION FEE & PAYMENT MODE:
- Fee payable: Rs. 100/- (Rupees One Hundred only).
- Women candidates and candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST) and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) eligible for reservation are exempted from payment of fee (Fee = Rs. 0).

6. HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1: Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the SSC official portal at https://ssc.gov.in.
Step 2: Fill the online application form with personal, category, and educational qualifications.
Step 3: Choose preferred CAPF force preference codes and examination city centers.
Step 4: Upload scanned photograph and signature according to prescribed dimensions.
Step 5: Pay application fee online via UPI, Net Banking, or Debit Card (if applicable).
Step 6: Submit and retain a printed copy of the confirmation page for physical standard test (PST/PET) stages.
