# National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS)

MINISTRY OF EDUCATION
Department of School Education & Literacy, Government of India

CENTRAL SECTOR SCHEME NOTIFICATION: National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) 2026-2027

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE:
To award scholarships to meritorious students of economically weaker sections to arrest their drop out at Class 8 and encourage them to continue their study at secondary stage (Classes 9 to 12). Under this scheme, 100,000 scholarships of Rs. 12,000 per annum (Rs. 1,000 per month) are awarded.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Class Enrolled: Students studying as regular students in Class 8 in Government, Local Body, and Government-aided schools.
- Minimum Marks: Minimum 55% marks or equivalent grade in Class 7 examination (5% relaxation for SC/ST students, i.e. 50%).
- Parental Income: Total annual parental income from all sources must not exceed Rs. 3,50,000 (3.5 Lakhs per annum).
- Category / Gender: Open to all categories (GEN, OBC, SC, ST) and all genders.
- Age Limit: Typically 12 to 16 years.

3. IMPORTANT DATES:
- Online Application Opens on NSP Portal: 01 October 2026
- Last Date for Application Submission: 30 November 2026
- First Level (Institute) Verification: 15 December 2026
- State Level Selection Examination Date: January 2027

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Class 7 Annual Report Card / Marksheet with official school stamp
- Parental Income Certificate issued by Revenue Authority [Lead time: 15 days]
- Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) where applicable [Lead time: 30 days]
- Disability Certificate (if applying under Divyang quota)
- Aadhaar Card and linked student bank account passbook

5. APPLICATION PROCESS:
- Mode: Online on National Scholarship Portal (NSP 2.0) at https://scholarships.gov.in.
- Application Fee: Nil (Rs. 0).

6. HOW TO APPLY:
Step 1: Register on National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in) with student Aadhaar or Aadhaar Enrolment ID.
Step 2: Fill state of domicile, scholarship category ('Pre-Matric / Secondary'), student name, date of birth, mobile number, and email.
Step 3: Select 'National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme' and enter Class 7 marks and school U-DISE code.
Step 4: Upload income certificate and caste certificate.
Step 5: Submit application online and provide a copy to the school Headmaster for NSP institutional verification.
