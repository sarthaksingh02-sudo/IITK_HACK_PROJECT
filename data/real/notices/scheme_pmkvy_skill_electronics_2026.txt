# Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY 4.0) - Special Skill Certification in Electronics & Mobile Hardware

NATIONAL SKILL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION (NSDC)
Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (MSDE), Government of India
Notice Ref: MSDE/PMKVY4.0/ELEC/2026-27

OFFICIAL ADMISSION NOTICE: Free Skill Training & Certification in Mobile Hardware Repair & Domestic Electronics Technician

1. PROGRAM SUMMARY:
Under the PMKVY 4.0 initiative, fully government-sponsored technical skill training courses are offered with NSQF certification, toolkit allowance, and post-placement apprenticeship support across district Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Kendras (PMKK).

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Minimum Education: 8th pass (Middle School) or 10th pass (Secondary School).
- Age Criteria: 15 to 45 years.
- Category / Gender: Open to all categories (GEN, OBC, SC, ST) and all genders. Special reservation quotas for rural youth.
- Previous Skill / Prior Experience: No prior technical experience required. Candidates with informal self-taught skills can opt for 1-day RPL (Recognition of Prior Learning) direct assessment.

3. IMPORTANT DATES:
- Enrollment Opens: 10 October 2026
- Application Last Date: 20 December 2026
- Batch Training Commencement: January 2027

4. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for biometric attendance)
- Class 8 or Class 10 Passing Certificate / Marksheet
- Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar linked for DBT stipend transfer)
- Two passport size photographs

5. COURSE FEES & STIPEND:
- Training Fee: 100% Free of Cost (Funded by Central Government).
- Certification: NSQF Level 3/4 Certificate issued by Electronics Sector Skills Council of India (ESSCI).
- Assessment Fee: Free of cost.

6. HOW TO ENROL:
Step 1: Visit Skill India Digital portal at https://www.skillindiadigital.gov.in or visit the nearest District PMKK center.
Step 2: Search for 'Field Technician - Computing and Peripherals' or 'Mobile Phone Hardware Technician' (Job Role: ELE/Q4601).
Step 3: Register with mobile OTP and verify Aadhaar credentials.
Step 4: Select preferred local training center and schedule counseling session.
Step 5: Attend in-person verification and collect student welcome kit.
